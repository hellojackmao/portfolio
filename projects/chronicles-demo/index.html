Controls & timing

Move: WASD, arrow keys or the direction buttons.
Action: Space or the Action button.
Dodge: Shift or the Dodge button.
Pause: Escape or Pause.

Attacks aim toward the nearest unrestored seal. A second tap within 260 ms becomes a projectile; holding for 650 ms prepares a charged strike. A single tap waits briefly to distinguish it from a double-tap.

A mechanics study, not the full game. This new top-down browser arena explores the concept’s input timing and feedback. It does not implement the proposed third-person adventure, narrative, character progression or final art.
